Maps first, technology second.

My work starts with a place. Relief and terrain, accessibility, settlement patterns, the quiet data problems behind a map. The tools exist to make those things legible, not the other way around.

The maps are made with QGIS, Blender, Python and open data. The web applications are small, focused tools built around a single geographic question.
